########################### Association Profiles ##################################

Delta Analytics was founded in 2014 by a group of economic consultants from the San Francisco Bay Area. Their primary background is in the private sector, but they sought to leverage their data skills for the welfare of the community by focusing on the non-profit sector. The organization collaborates with non-profits to generate positive social impact through key data insights and management services. Their mission is encapsulated in the phrase "data for change," reflecting their dedication to using data to drive social progress. Delta Analytics helps public service organizations with various data-driven needs, including compiling metrics, managing databases, and producing statistical inferences, all aimed at facilitating the organization, measurement, and creation of social change.

To be a member of the Delta Analytics Fellow, you need to meet the following criteria:

1. **Deep Technical Knowledge**: You should have a strong background in data science, engineering, or data visualization.
2. **Professional Experience**: You might be working as an economic consultant, data scientist, data analyst, or software engineer at a technology firm. Alternatively, you could have data experience from schooling (e.g., economics or statistics programs), research projects, or internships.
3. **Passion for Nonprofits**: You should have a demonstrated passion for assisting nonprofits and a commitment to Delta's mission.
4. **Time Commitment**: You must be able to commit 5-10 hours per week for a 6-month period, with monthly cohort-wide Saturday working sessions.
5. **Remote Work**: While Delta is based in the US, they accept remote fellows nationally.
6. **Educational Background**: Full-time undergraduate students are not eligible, but students enrolled in Masters, PhD, or professional programs (e.g., MBA) with data experience can apply.

These criteria ensure that fellows have the necessary technical skills, professional experience, and dedication to make a meaningful impact in the nonprofit sector.       

The Delta Analytics Fellow is a highly competitive program. According to the provided context, in 2017, only 25-35 fellows were selected from thousands of applications. This indicates that being chosen as a Delta Fellow is a significant achievement, reflecting a high level of recognition and reputation within the field.

########################### Membership Requirements ##################################

The specific criteria for membership in the Delta Analytics Fellow program are as follows:

1. **Previous Data and Analytics Experience**: Applicants should have some experience working with data, which can come from professional work, schooling (e.g., economics or statistics programs), research projects, or internships. The range of data experience can vary widely, and there is no definitive list of required skills.

2. **Passion for Assisting Nonprofits**: Demonstrated enthusiasm and commitment to helping nonprofits is crucial.

3. **Professional Background**: Typical applicants are professionals who have worked with data in various capacities, such as economic consultants, data analysts or data scientists at tech companies, data visualization specialists, economics PhDs, program managers, software engineers, and financial analysts.

4. **Commitment to Mission**: A strong commitment to Delta Analytics' mission and an interest in applying and expanding data capabilities.

5. **Eligibility for Students**: Full-time undergraduate students are not eligible. However, students enrolled in a Masters, PhD, or professional program (e.g., MBA) who have data experience are eligible to apply.

Additionally, since 2022, the program has been accepting remote fellows nationally, not just those in the San Francisco Bay Area.
