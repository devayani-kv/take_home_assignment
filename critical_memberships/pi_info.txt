########################### Association Profiles ##################################

Pi Ventures began its journey by investing in AI (artificial intelligence) startups and has since expanded its focus to support disruptive innovations across deeptech, including hardware companies like the space rocket venture Agnikul. The firm aims to be the first institutional investor in startups, although they sometimes come in as the second investor. This early-stage investment strategy is a core part of their mission.

Manish Singhal, the founding partner of Pi Ventures, has a rich background in technology, starting with a degree from IIT-Kanpur in 1992 and extensive experience in video compression and various roles from techie to VC partner. Pi Ventures has made around 22 investments through its two funds, with the current fund being about $85 million.

Singhal highlights a significant issue facing the Indian startup ecosystem: the intellectual property drain. Many tech startups prefer to register overseas, leading to a situation where IP created in India is captured by headquarters outside the country. This is a critical problem that needs urgent attention to ensure the growth and sustainability of India's startup ecosystem.

The membership criteria to be a member of Pi Ventures Fellow is being one of 30 fellows selected from thousands of applications, indicating that it is a highly competitive program.      

The Pi Ventures Fellow program is highly competitive and prestigious. In 2020, only 30 fellows were selected from thousands of applications, indicating a rigorous selection process and a high level of recognition for those who are chosen. This fellowship is considered a significant achievement and is indicative of the fellow's exceptional capabilities and potential in their field.

########################### Membership Requirements ##################################

The specific criteria for membership in the Pi Ventures Fellow programme are as follows:

1. **Industry Experience**: Applicants must have 10 years or more of industry experience.
2. **Educational Background**: An engineering degree is mandatory.
3. **Professional Background**: Candidates should have a background in product, technology, or investing.
4. **Startup Experience**: Experience as a founder or an early team member in a startup is a significant plus.
5. **Consulting Background**: While not mandatory, a consulting background is beneficial.
6. **Passion for Technology**: A deep passion for technology-enabled innovations is essential.

The programme is designed for senior professionals who aspire to take on senior roles such as Principals, Managing Directors, or Partners in a venture fund, or those looking to launch their own funds. The cohort is limited to 12 individuals to ensure a personalized experience, and there will be video calls with shortlisted applicants to finalize the selection.
