########################### Association Profiles ##################################

YEH (Young Entrepreneur's Hub) is a global founder-focused membership community based in Silicon Valley. It is a free, purpose-built platform designed for high-integrity entrepreneurs to network, launch, join, and grow businesses. The community caters to startups, small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs), creators, and international businesses. YEH organizes high-participation events globally, features entrepreneurs weekly, and facilitates connections between founders and essential contacts such as venture capitalists and cofounders.

The mission of YEH is threefold:
1. **To bring the community together** through events, activities, and online channels.
2. **To platform the community** by providing a crowdsourced incubator/accelerator, enabling members to start at 25% rather than 0%.
3. **To ensure members succeed or fail for the right reasons**, avoiding unnecessary obstacles.

YEH aims to address common challenges in entrepreneurship, such as the difficulty and loneliness of the journey, and the prevalence of failure due to avoidable reasons. The organization engages its members directly with business opportunities across its growing international hub locations, including live hubs in San Francisco/Silicon Valley, USA, and Sydney, Australia, with more locations planned globally.

For more information, you can visit their [website](https://linktr.ee/young.entrepreneurs.hub).

The membership criteria to be a member of YEH (Young Entrepreneur's Hub) is selective membership by application. It is a Global Founder-Focused Membership Community based in Silicon Valley.
