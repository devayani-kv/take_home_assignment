########################### Association Profiles ##################################

The Stanford Web3 & AI Research Group, also known as the Stanford AI & Web3 Research Lab Association (SAIW3RL), was founded in 2020 by a group of professors and students at Stanford University. They observed the rapid growth of the crypto industry and identified that many new crypto initiatives struggled with securing initial resources, including funding and technology development. To address these challenges, they established the Stanford Blockchain Accelerator to provide funding, mentorship, and resources to promising projects, thereby fostering innovation and supporting the growth of the decentralized ecosystem.

The mission of the Stanford Web3 & AI Research Group is to offer expertise from professionals working to build Web3, inform and drive policy and regulatory agendas, and bridge the gap between the blockchain industry and academia. They aim to combine, synchronize, and leverage blockchain-related activities across corporations, startups, venture capitalists, and scientific institutions. Additionally, they focus on cultivating professional workflows between technologists, public policymakers, and specialists, advancing best standards, benchmarks, and best practices in blockchain technology, and serving as a neutral body to aggregate and coordinate blockchain activities.

The membership criteria to be a member of the Stanford Web3 & AI Research Group is selective membership by application.

#################### Membership Requirements ##########################

Stanford Ai & Web3 Research Lab follows a strict "unicorns only" rule when judging potential new members. A "unicorn" is a high-achieving individual who will add value to our network.

The membership criteria to be a Stanford AI and Web3 Research Group member involve a selective process by application. Members may take on roles such as judge, speaker, and mentor.
