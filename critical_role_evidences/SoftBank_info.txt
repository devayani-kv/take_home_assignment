*************************** Organization Profiles and Achievements *****************************

SoftBank has a rich history and a clear mission statement that guides its operations and strategic direction.

### History
- **Founding and Early Years**: SoftBank was founded by Masayoshi Son, who studied at the University of California, Berkeley, and immersed himself in various inventions and businesses.
- **2002**: J-Phone Co., Ltd., a part of SoftBank, launched the first W-CDMA third-generation mobile telecom service in the world compliant with 3GPP standards.
- **2003**: SoftBank BB Corp. was established through the merger of BB Technologies Corporation, SOFTBANK Networks Inc., SOFTBANK EC HOLDINGS CORP., and SOFTBANK COMMERCE CORP.
- **2014**: Alibaba Group Holding Limited, in which SoftBank had invested, listed on the New York Stock Exchange.
- **2015**: The company name of SoftBank Corp. was changed to SoftBank Group Corp. to clearly identify its role as a holding company driving future global development.    
- **2018**: SoftBank Corp. listed on the First Section of the Tokyo Stock Exchange.
- **2021**: SoftBank Group Corp. relocated its headquarters to the Tokyo Portcity Takeshiba Office Tower.
- **2023**: Arm, a company under SoftBank, went public, raising $4.87 billion at a $54.5 billion valuation, with SoftBank continuing to own 90.6% of the company following the offering.

### Mission Statement
SoftBank's mission is encapsulated in its corporate philosophy and vision:
- **Philosophy**: "Information Revolution — Happiness for everyone." Since its founding, SoftBank has sought to promote the Information Revolution to contribute to the wellbeing of people and society.
- **Vision**: "Becoming a Corporate Group needed most by people around the world." The aim is to contribute to people’s happiness through the Information Revolution and become the corporate group needed most by people globally.

### Strategic Vision
- **Next 30-Year Vision**: In 2010, SoftBank unveiled "SoftBank's Next 30-Year Vision," which looks 100 years into the future. The strategy focuses on meeting society's most essential needs through a three-pronged approach, aiming to power the world's most essential technologies.

### Sustainability
SoftBank is committed to solving social issues with digital technology to realize a sustainable society. The group has identified six material issues to engage with in its business under the concept of “a world where all things, information, and hearts are connected.”

This comprehensive history and mission statement reflect SoftBank's long-term commitment to innovation, societal contribution, and sustainability.
